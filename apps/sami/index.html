01Preview

See it before you keep it

Point a task at your Photos library, an album or any folder, even on a NAS. Slide between the original and the optimized copy — nothing changes until you say so.

02Visually lossless

Smaller, and it looks the same

Tick one box and Sami tries several qualities on each photo and video, keeping the smallest that still looks like the original. If none does, the original stays.

03Toolbox

More than an optimizer.

Convert and combine files, tag films and shows with artwork and chapters, take Live Photos apart, and record Flash movies at up to 4K.

04Automation

Then it runs itself

Schedule a task daily, weekly or monthly, or run it from Shortcuts and Siri. It can keep a backup of every original, too.

05The alternatives

Why not use a website, or buy more iCloud?

Online compressors upload your media to someone else's server. A bigger iCloud plan keeps every file at full size, and bills you every month.

Yes, Sami uses lossy compression to achieve significant space savings. There will be some quality loss, but the side-by-side preview feature lets you evaluate the quality trade-off before committing. Savings vary based on your media and chosen quality settings, but typically range from 30-70% for photos and videos. Sami provides estimated savings before you optimize, so you know exactly what to expect. PDFs can often be reduced by 50% or more depending on their content.

Sami can optionally keep reference versions depending on your settings, but you should always maintain your own backups of important media before optimization. The compression is permanent, so backing up originals elsewhere is recommended for files you may want at full quality in the future.
