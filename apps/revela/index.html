The honest way

Four steps. No shortcuts.

Honest Mode means no preview, no switching stocks mid-roll, and a real wait while the lab does its work. That's not a limitation — that's the whole point.

1

Load a roll

Pick a box from the humming mint fridge. Tear the foil, drop the canister in, thread the leader. No peeking now.

2

Shoot it blind

A viewfinder, a wind lever, a frame counter. Every frame counts because you can't see a single one of them yet.

3

Mail it off

Rewind, drop the canister in the mailer, and wait like everyone used to. Rush it in an hour — or send it away for days.

4

Tear it open

Your envelope lands on the shelf. Flip it, pull the seal, and meet your photographs for the very first time.

More than stills

Reels, projector nights, a living studio

Movie reels

Cine stocks load as fixed-length reels at 24 fps — or a Super-8-style 18 — developed frame by frame with optional warm vinyl-character sound.

Projector night

AirPlay your finished rolls to the TV as a true second screen: slide-carousel clunks, projector fan hum, the whole family gathered around.

A lived-in studio

Shelves of photo boxes down a corridor, a calendar with reveal days circled in pen, a radio that plays, plants that grow the more you shoot — and a dog underfoot, eventually a cat on the shelf.
