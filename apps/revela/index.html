Pick a Box from the Fridge

Twelve stocks spanning seven decades, from slide film to movie reels. Load one, and in Honest Mode it stays loaded until the roll is done.

02Shoot

Shoot it blind

A viewfinder, a wind lever, a frame counter. Every frame counts because you can't see a single one of them yet.

03Wait

Mail it off

Rewind, drop the canister in the mailer, and wait like everyone used to. Rush it in an hour — or send it away for days.

04Reveal

An envelope worth waiting for

Your envelope lands on the shelf. Flip it, pull the seal, and meet your photographs for the very first time.

05Develop

The Lab

Keep shooting and a red glow appears at the end of the hall. Behind that door, develop photos from your own library into any stock.
