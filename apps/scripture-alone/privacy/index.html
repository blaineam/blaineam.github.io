Scripture Alone · Privacy

Your Bible, your notes, your device

Scripture Alone collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours. This page says exactly what stays on your device and the few times anything leaves it — always because you asked.

Applies to iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS · Updated September 2026
In short

What the developer receives: nothing

No data collected

The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind, and it sends nothing about you to the developer. There is no server to send it to.

No account

Nothing asks you to sign in. Every feature works without one.

Open source

The complete source is public under the AGPL, so anyone can check that this page is true: github.com/blaineam/Scripture-Alone.

On your device

What stays with you

Your highlights, notes, favorites, reading position, settings and any translations you add are stored on your device. On Apple devices they sync between your own devices through your private iCloud database, which Apple encrypts and the developer cannot read. On Android they stay on the device; to move them to a new phone, export them or make a keepsake file.

A photo of a sermon slide is read on the device to start a note. It is never uploaded, and it is kept only if you choose "Keep this photo with the note".

Keys you enter for the ESV or API.Bible are encrypted on the device (the Keychain on Apple devices, the Android Keystore on Android) and backed up only through your own iCloud Keychain or Google Block Store, end-to-end encrypted.

Network

The only times anything leaves your device

  • Study add-ons and translations shipped with the app download from Apple's or Google Play's own servers the first time you open them.
  • Browse Free Translations fetches eBible.org's public catalogue, and a translation only when you pick one. No identifier is sent.
  • Online translations (ESV, API.Bible), if you add your own key, request the chapter you are reading from that publisher's service with your key. Their privacy policies govern those requests.
  • Share links carry the passage inside the link itself, after the #, which browsers never send to a server; opening one loads the Scripture Alone web page like any other page.
  • Listen uses the voices on your device. On Android, a voice that reads over the network sends the text to Google to be spoken; for a translation whose licence doesn't allow handing text to other software, those voices are not used. On iPhone, choosing a Mi Speaks Studio voice hands the chapter to the Mi Speaks app on the same device.
  • Feedback is by email, only if you write.

On Android, the on-device text recognizer (Google ML Kit) would ordinarily upload diagnostics to Google; the app removes the component that sends them, so nothing is uploaded.

Everything else

Children, changes and contact

Scripture Alone is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone. If this policy ever changes, this page will say so and carry a new date. Questions: blaine@wemiller.com. For visiting this website or writing to the developer, the general privacy policy applies.
