📖 Scripture Alone

The Word, and nothing in the way.

A free, open-source Bible for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with its own Apple Watch app. The American Standard Version is on your device from the start and two more translations are a single download away, so you can read, study, listen and take notes anywhere in the world — with or without a connection.

A quiet place to read

Everything here works today, on the device in your hand.

📖

The whole Bible, offline

The American Standard Version is on your device from the moment the app installs, so it reads offline before you ever connect. The Berean Standard Bible and the King James Version download once, the first time you choose one, and are yours offline from then on. All three are public domain, with full-text search.

📜

Set the way it was written

The words of Christ in red, section headings, poetry laid out as poetry, and footnotes a tap away.

🔤

Reading comfort

Seven typefaces, adjustable size and line spacing, five themes — Auto, Light, Sepia, Dark and Black — and an accent color of your choosing. Read in paragraphs or verse by verse, with full Dynamic Type support.

🙌

Hands-free reading

Auto-scroll keeps the text moving at your pace and carries on into the next chapter — an accessibility feature for anyone who finds scrolling by hand difficult, or simply wants to read without touching the screen.

🧭

Go straight there

Type “jn 3 16” or “rom 8:28-39” to jump to a passage, or search for any word or phrase. Recent chapters and recent searches are waiting when you come back.

🖍️

Highlights, notes and favorites

Highlight in five colors, heart the verses you return to, and attach a note to one or more passages — a Sunday sermon on Romans 8:1–17, say. Export your notes as a PDF, Markdown or plain text.

📷

Camera notes

Photograph the sermon slide and on-device text recognition starts your note: titled from the slide, linked to every passage on it, its points ready as bullets. The photo is discarded unless you keep it.

🎧

Listen

Hear any chapter read aloud with your device's voices, Premium and Personal Voice included, the spoken verse marked as it goes, with a sleep timer and lock-screen controls. On iPhone and iPad, Mi Speaks subscribers can hand a chapter to its Studio voices.

🔎

Study mode

A toggle away: tap a verse and the Study panel follows, with ranked cross-references (one tap there and back) and classic commentary from John Calvin, John Gill and Jamieson‑Fausset‑Brown. The commentary downloads the first time you open it; cross-references are there from the start.

🗺️

Maps, timeline and charts

Each chapter's era on a timeline of the biblical periods, an offline map of the places it names, and charts of the kings of Israel and Judah, Paul's journeys, the twelve tribes and the feasts of Israel.

🖼️

Verse images worth sharing

Design a verse image on your device from eight templates, or send a link that rebuilds the card in any browser from the link itself — nothing is stored on a server.

⌚

Widgets and Apple Watch

Verse of the Day and Favorites & Notes widgets for the Home and Lock Screens, and an Apple Watch app with the ASV, BSB and KJV offline — it follows the translation you're reading on your iPhone — plus your favorites and notes, and a complication with the day's reference.

🕯️

Legacy Bible

Make a keepsake of your highlights and notes for your family: one file, optionally protected with a passphrase, that they open and read as you marked it — a digital “Dad's Bible” to hand down.

☁️

Private iCloud sync

Your highlights, notes and favorites follow you across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch through your own private iCloud. No account, no server — I never see your data.

🔓

Open source

Every line of code is public under the AGPL-3.0 license. Read it, check it, or build it yourself.

📚

More translations

Dozens more are free from eBible.org, downloaded only when you choose one, and you can open a USFM file or an ePub you already own. The ESV, CSB, NKJV and NASB can't be given away by anyone, so they're read with your own free key from the publisher. Licenses to carry those offline are still being sought.

🔐

Encrypted, and provably so

The American Standard Version ships sealed: signed by a key the app pins, decrypted a chapter at a time, searchable without its text ever lying in the open on your device. It's the format a publisher's own translation would travel in — read how it works.

⚖️

Two translations at once

Put any two side by side, verse for verse, and see where they part company — including a translation you read online with your own key.

🔠

The words underneath

Tap a verse for the Hebrew or Greek behind it, word by word, with parsing, Strong's numbers and a lexicon entry for each. It works in every translation, and downloads once the first time you open it.

📥

Bring your notes with you

Years of notes in another Bible app don't have to be left behind. Import a Life Bible (formerly Tecarta) export directly, or paste notes or a CSV from anywhere else. Importing twice is safe — anything already there is left alone.

👨‍👩‍👧

Live family sharing

Share your notes and highlights with family as you keep adding to them, not only as a keepsake file.
