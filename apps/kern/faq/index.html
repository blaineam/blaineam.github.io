Gameplay

Every puzzle is generated with a solver-computed par. Reaching par is worth 10,000 points and finishing faster earns up to 1,000 more, with the identical formula in all three modes. Harder boards allow more time, so no world, level or mode can tilt the leaderboard.

The puzzles are in English: every board is built from an English word list, and every word you play is an English word. The menus, tutorials and help are in nine languages — English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese and Italian.

About Kern

Kerning is the typographer's craft of adjusting the space between letter pairs until a word sits perfectly — the most obsessive, invisible detail in type. A word game named Kern promises letter-level care. And if you squeeze the r and n too close together, "kerning" reads as "keming" — which is why playing a word with r-n in it earns an achievement by that name.
