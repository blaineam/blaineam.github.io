FAQ

Every puzzle is generated with a solver-computed par. Reaching par is worth 10,000 points and finishing faster earns up to 1,000 more, with the identical formula in all three modes. Harder boards allow more time, so no world, level or mode can tilt the leaderboard.

No — reactivity is cosmetic by law. Worlds change the light, the tiles, the music, and the flourishes, never the formula. Finding a word that belongs to a world feeds achievements and your per-world journal, not your score.

One seeded puzzle per mode per day, derived from the date itself — so the whole world plays the same boards with no server involved. Your first completion per mode posts to that day's Game Center leaderboards, and free play stays endless alongside it.

No to both. Kern is $4.99 once — no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no data collection. Seasonal worlds arrive as part of the game, not as events or passes.

It's generated on your device — there are no looping tracks. Each world has a recipe (scale, tempo, instruments), your letter selections walk up the scale, and a valid word resolves a chord sized by its length. It honors the silent switch, and you can mix with your own audio from Settings.

Kerning is the typographer's craft of adjusting the space between letter pairs until a word sits perfectly — the most obsessive, invisible detail in type. A word game named Kern promises letter-level care. And if you squeeze the r and n too close together, "kerning" reads as "keming" — which is why playing a word with r-n in it earns an achievement by that name.

The puzzles are in English: every board is built from an English word list, and every word you play is an English word. The menus, tutorials and help are in nine languages — English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese and Italian.
