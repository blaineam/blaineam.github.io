Three modes. One fair score.

Wheel, Search, and Chain share a single par-based formula — so the daily leaderboard is one competition, not three.

Mode 01

Wheel

Swipe through a letter wheel to spell words that fill a mini crossword. Extra words bank as bonus finds.

Mode 02

Search

A word search with a twist: every hidden word belongs to the world you're in. An Ocean board hides ocean words.

Mode 03

Chain

Drag any path through neighboring letters and build your own words. The board hides more than you think.

Pick a place, not a palette

Every world has its own light, its own tiles, and its own generated music — and it listens. Long words pulse the board, streaks build the world's energy, and a word that belongs to the world earns its signature flourish.

Seasonal worlds join the picker and the daily rotation on their own calendar — no downloads, no events to sign up for. They simply appear when their season does, and step back when it passes.

One score. No favorites.

Every puzzle carries a computed par, and your score is your quality against par plus a speed bonus — the same formula in every mode, every world, every level. No theme or setting can buy points. That's what makes the daily leaderboard one competition instead of three.

  • ⏸️

    A pause that hides the board

    The pause button stops the clock and covers the board, so a pause buys time, never thinking time. Kern also pauses itself when a call or notification pulls you away.

  • 💡

    Hints and definitions

    Each puzzle gives you three hints that show a word you can still find. Tap any word you've found to read its dictionary definition, offline.

Built the quiet way

A daily ritual that respects your time — no server, no events, no strings.

  • 📅

    The Daily Kern

    One seeded puzzle per mode per day — the same boards for everyone on Earth, computed on your device. No server involved, nothing to download.

  • 🏆

    Daily leaderboards

    Game Center daily boards for each mode, plus a combined board that sums all three. Lifetime scores count in Ems — an em is type's own unit of measure.

  • 😉

    Achievements with jokes

    From First Ligature to Em Dash to Full Bleed — and yes, playing a word with consecutive r-n in it unlocks one called Keming.

  • 🎵

    Music that never loops

    Every session composes itself from the world's own recipe: letters walk the scale, a valid word resolves the chord. No two sessions sound alike.

  • ✈️

    Fully offline

    Dictionary, puzzle generation, scoring, worlds, and music all live on device. Airplane mode is a perfectly good place to play.

  • 📱

    iPhone + iPad

    Universal, in every orientation — Split View, Slide Over, and Stage Manager included. The board scales, the wheel re-anchors, nothing clips.

Set in quiet type

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Puzzles, scoring, worlds, and music are all computed locally, and the whole game works offline. The only thing that ever leaves is a Game Center score you choose to post.
