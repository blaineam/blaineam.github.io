A starting point, not a finish line.

01Match

Borrow the look you love

Pick a few photos as your styles, then develop a whole shoot at once. Luma matches each picture to the style that suits it best.

02Develop

A real editor underneath

Every match arrives as sliders, tone curves and an eight-band colour mixer you can keep editing. RAW develops from the full sensor data.

03Masks

Change just part of it

Gradients, light and colour ranges, or the subject and background found on your device. Each mask gets its own full set of adjustments.

04Retouch

Tap a blemish. It's gone.

Luma rebuilds the spot from the texture around it. Each repair stays an adjustment you can move or remove a month later.

05Cull

Keep the best of the burst

Luma groups near-duplicates and suggests the keeper by focus, open eyes and exposure. Picks go to a Luma Keepers album, and nothing is ever deleted.
