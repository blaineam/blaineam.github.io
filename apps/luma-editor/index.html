Luma 2.0 — A Real Editor

A style match used to end in pixels. Now it arrives as adjustments you own, with a whole editor around them.

🎚️

Every Match Becomes Sliders

Exposure, Brilliance, Highlights, Shadows, Contrast, Black Point, White Point, Saturation, Vibrance, Warmth, Tint, Sharpness, Definition, Noise Reduction, Vignette and Grain — plus four-channel tone curves, an eight-band colour mixer and split toning. Intensity now scales the adjustments themselves, so every slider stays meaningful at any setting.

🖼️

Crop, Straighten, Lens

Crop by dragging the corners, straighten by hand or let Luma find the horizon on device, correct the perspective of a building shot from below, and fix what the glass did — radial distortion, chromatic aberration and colour fringing. Rotate and flip in quarter turns.

🩹

Retouch and Masks

Tap a blemish and it goes, with clean skin or sky found nearby to replace it — and stored as an adjustment, so you can move it or take it away a month later. Masks put an adjustment on part of the photo instead of all of it: gradients you drag on the image, luminance and colour ranges, or the subject and the people, found on device.

🗂️

Cull a Burst, Edit a Clip

Point Luma at a burst and it groups the near-duplicates and suggests the keeper, weighing focus against whether eyes are open. Picks are filed into a Luma Keepers album, best first, so you can find them in Photos — and nothing is ever deleted. Video edits the same way a photo does, straight from the Photos editing screen.

Why Choose Luma Editor

A Look You Love, and the Controls to Change It

Start from a photo whose grade you admire — your own or someone else's — and finish it your way.

🎨

Match Your Style

Save the photos whose look you want as styles. Luma studies one, works out how to get your photo there, and applies it — so a whole shoot can come out looking like your last one, or like the photographer you are learning from.

🎚️

Not a Filter — Adjustments

A match hands you real sliders, not a strength dial. Light, Color, Detail and Effects, four-channel tone curves, an eight-band colour mixer, split toning, and a proper black-and-white conversion where the mixer acts as the channel mix. Undo and redo step through your edits one at a time.

🎭

Masks

Adjust part of the photo instead of all of it. Linear and radial gradients you place by dragging, luminance and colour ranges, and Subject, Background and People found on device. Each mask gets its own complete set of adjustments — and a style can be matched to one mask alone, your subject against their subject.

🖼️

Crop, Straighten, Perspective

Drag the corners with a thirds grid and aspect presets. Straighten by hand or find the horizon automatically. Correct the perspective of something tall shot from below. Lens distortion, chromatic aberration and defringe handle what the glass did.

🩹

Retouch

Tap a blemish and it goes. Luma finds clean skin or sky nearby to replace it with, and you can drag the source elsewhere if you disagree. Spots are stored as adjustments rather than baked into the picture, so a repair can be moved, resized or undone any time.

🗂️

Cull a Burst

Hand Luma a burst and it groups the near-duplicates and suggests which to keep, measuring focus, whether eyes are open, and what is clipped or flat. The picks land in a Luma Keepers album, best first. The choice stays yours, and nothing is ever deleted.

A Starting Point, Not a Finish Line

Luma studies the photo you picked — its tone, its colour, how it handles shadows and highlights — and solves for the adjustments that get your photo there. What you get back is the recipe, not the result.

So the interesting part starts where a filter would stop. Warm it a little further, pull the highlights back, lift only the blues, or match the look to your subject and leave the background alone. It is your edit from the first tap.

Bulk Edit with Precision

Develop a whole shoot at once and Luma matches each photo to the closest style, so an afternoon's work comes out consistent without a preset being forced onto pictures it does not suit.

Save a look as a named preset, or copy one photo's look and paste it onto the next. Presets leave the framing behind — a crop belongs to the photo it was drawn on. Auto Enhance is there when you just want somewhere to start.

Editable Later, Inside Photos

Luma edits in place the way Photos does. Your original is never touched, Revert to Original always works, edits sync through iCloud Photos, and reopening a photo brings back every slider exactly where you left it — not a flattened picture to start over from.

Luma adds itself to the Photos editing screen too: tap Edit, then the extensions button, then Luma, and the whole editor is there on that photo — or on that video. Or take a look elsewhere entirely, as Camera Raw settings for Lightroom or as a 3D LUT.
