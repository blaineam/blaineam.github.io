Zero in minutes.

No guesswork, and no box of ammo spent chasing a zero.

01Print

Print a Coded Target

Print a uniquely coded target on plain paper, from Letter to print-shop sheets. Your first target is free; after that, each one uses a print credit, an in-app purchase.

02Scan

Snap a Photo, Get Your Clicks

Photograph your group, even at an angle. On-device vision finds every hole and tells you exactly how many clicks of windage and elevation to dial.

03Setup

Rifle, Pistol & Bow

MOA or MIL, yards or meters, from scopes to pistol red dots. Save a profile for every setup, and with a bow it tells you which way to move your sight.

SightQuick is free to download, and your first target is free. After that, each target uses a print credit, sold as an in-app purchase in packs of 1, 5 or 20. A credit covers one complete zeroing session you can re-photograph as you dial in. No subscription, no ads.
