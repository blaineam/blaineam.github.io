SightQuick

Questions and answers

Everything shooters ask about SightQuick, from printing the first target to how it keeps your photos private.

Getting started

No. SightQuick generates the target for you and you print it on plain paper with any home or office printer — Letter, A4, or Tabloid. Each target is uniquely coded so the app knows its exact size and geometry. There's nothing to order, ship, or store.

You photograph the target and SightQuick uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to read the QR code, straighten the page off its printed border, and locate every bullet hole against the orange aim point. It runs entirely on your phone — no internet required — and shows the detected shots so you can confirm or fine-tune them before it calculates your adjustment.

Rifle scopes and pistol red dots with MOA or MIL clicks, at any distance you set in yards or meters (great for close-range red-dot work too). SightQuick converts your group into exact clicks, and a two-group calibration corrects for optics that don't track perfectly to their marked clicks. For archery there are no clicks — it measures your group and tells you which way and how far to move your sight, in inches or centimeters.

SightQuick is an iPhone app, tuned for a phone you can bring to the bench or the range. It uses the camera to photograph your target and runs on iOS 26 or later.

Credits and ranges

SightQuick is free to download, and your first target is free. After that, each target uses a print credit, sold as an in-app purchase in packs of 1, 5 or 20. A credit covers one complete zeroing session you can re-photograph as you dial in. No subscription, no ads.

Yes. Print a numbered load-development ladder, shoot a group per charge weight, and scan the whole sheet in one photo. SightQuick ranks the groups and highlights your accuracy node. A ladder uses two print credits.

Yes. Ranges can order packs of uniquely-coded SightQuick targets to print and sell. Each one is cryptographically signed, so a customer just scans it to activate — free, fully offline, and locked to their device. Counterfeit, voided, or copied targets are detected and rejected. Interested in stocking SightQuick targets? Reach out via the support page.

Privacy and availability

Completely. Your photos are analyzed on-device and never uploaded anywhere. There's no account and no tracking — your zeroing sessions and rifle profiles live only on your phone. Purchases are handled by Apple; SightQuick never sees your payment details.

Yes. SightQuick is on the App Store everywhere in the European Union, France included. It's available in every App Store territory Apple offers.
