Ridgeshot

Press. Settle. Break.

A long-range marksman game for iPhone and iPad, with honest ballistics out to 800 yards.

One shot. One breath. One ridgeline lit by the last hour of daylight.

01Scope

Pick Your Holdover

A second-focal-plane scope with a Christmas-tree reticle, marked from 100 out to 800 yards. Tap the hash for your range and hold it on the target.

02Ballistics

Real Bullet Drop

Past 300 yards the round falls, and the wind pushes it sideways. Read the flag, settle your breath, and break the shot between breaths.

03Targets

Paper, steel or wildlife

Shoot Olympic rings, steel plates and bottles, or 3D wildlife that walks the range. Animal targets unlock only after a one-time adult age check.

04Compete

One Bubble at a Time

Send a match through iMessage and your friend shoots inside the bubble. Or hand the phone across the couch, or find an opponent on Game Center.
