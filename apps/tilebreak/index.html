The Mahjong solitaire that never deals you a lost cause.

01Always solvable

Every deal can be won

Every board is dealt from a simulated play-through, so it can always be cleared. Even a shuffle mid-game re-rolls into a solvable layout.

02Two modes

Classic, or a coffee break

Classic is the full 144-tile set across seven layouts. Arcade is sixteen shorter boards with bigger tiles, sized for a quick clear.

03Themes

Pick a layout. Pick a style.

Six themes and four tile face sets, mixed however you like. Each theme plays its own generated music and switches the moment you do.

04Scoring

A real challenge, not a slot machine.

Combo streaks multiply your score up to 5×, and a hint or a misaimed tap breaks the streak. The end screen puts your personal best beside every stat.

05Game Center

Six leaderboards, 19 achievements

Scores are split by mode, so every board ranks fairly. Achievements are kept on your device first, so they count even if you never sign in.

Tap two matching tiles to remove them. A tile is "free" if nothing is stacked on top of it and at least one of its left or right sides is open. Most tiles need an exact match — under American rules, any flower matches any flower and any season matches any season.
