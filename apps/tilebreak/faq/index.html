Frequently Asked Questions

Every question about Tilebreak, answered.

Tap two matching tiles to remove them. A tile is "free" if nothing is stacked on top of it and at least one of its left or right sides is open. Most tiles need an exact match — under American rules, any flower matches any flower and any season matches any season.

Yes. Each board is generated by simulating a valid play-through first (the reverse-removal method) and then dealing the tiles into the resulting order. Even after a shuffle mid-game, the new layout is regenerated to be solvable.

American (the default) follows the modern western convention: any flower matches any flower, any season matches any season. Strict requires an exact identity match for every tile — and because flowers/seasons only ship as singles, Strict mode drops them from the pool entirely and substitutes extra winds so every tile has a partner.
