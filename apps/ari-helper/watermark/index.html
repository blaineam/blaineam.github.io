How the check works

  1. 1

    Woven into the picture

    Ari nudges the fine detail of every small square of the image, not its metadata, so a re-save doesn't strip it.

  2. 2

    Read back here

    This page reads those squares with the same arithmetic, in your browser, and counts how many agree.

  3. 3

    A clear answer

    When enough squares agree they spell out Ari's fixed pattern, which ordinary pictures never do by chance.

What a result means A mark that is found is reliable: it carries a checksum, so a picture reporting Ari's mark really does carry it. A mark that is not found proves much less. Resizing a picture removes it, heavy compression can, and a picture made almost entirely of flat colour may never have carried one — Ari only marks the parts of a picture with enough detail to hide a mark, and leaves flat colour untouched. The identifier shown is 16 bits, so it tells two marked pictures apart but is not unique: among a few hundred, two will eventually share one. It is also not a signature — it shows a picture came from Ari, not who made it.
