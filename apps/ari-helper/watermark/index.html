A mark that is found is reliable: the pattern it looks for does not occur by chance, so a picture reporting Ari's mark really does carry it. A mark that is not found proves much less. Heavy compression, cropping, resizing and screenshots all destroy it, and this says nothing about pictures from any other tool. It is also not a signature — it shows a picture came from Ari, not who made it.
