How the check works

  1. 1

    Woven into the picture

    Ari nudges the fine detail of every small square of the image, not its metadata, so a re-save doesn't strip it.

  2. 2

    Read back here

    This page reads those squares with the same arithmetic, in your browser, and counts how many agree.

  3. 3

    A clear answer

    When enough squares agree they spell out Ari's fixed pattern, which ordinary pictures never do by chance.

What a result means A mark that is found is reliable: the pattern it looks for does not occur by chance, so a picture reporting Ari's mark really does carry it. A mark that is not found proves much less. Cropping alone and resizing alone are survivable, but both together, shrinking a picture very far, or heavy compression will destroy it, and this says nothing about pictures from any other tool. It is also not a signature — it shows a picture came from Ari, not who made it.
