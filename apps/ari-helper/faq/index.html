Turn on Keep looking and it follows along as you move. With an on-device model, nothing leaves your iPhone.

Conversations sync across all your Apple devices via iCloud. Start a chat on your iPhone and continue it on your iPad or Mac seamlessly. When two of your devices are awake on the same Wi-Fi, Ari also hands the conversation straight across the local network, so it lands in about a second instead of waiting for the next iCloud round trip.

The pictures and files in a conversation travel the same way, not just their names—the receiving device asks only for what it's missing, and every file is verified against a hash before it's kept. Anything over 16 MB is deliberately left to iCloud, which resumes and survives a quit. iCloud remains the system of record either way: a device that's asleep, on cellular, or on another network catches up exactly as it always has. If you'd rather everything went through the cloud, the local link can be switched off in Settings → API Settings → Direct Device Link.

Photo Studio

Photo Studio removes objects you brush over, repairs scratches and creases, reduces noise, sharpens slight camera shake, adds color to black-and-white photos and enlarges pictures four times. It can also repaint a selection from a description. Film Studio renders a film shot by shot with the video model you choose, and cleans up any video: de-interlacing, grain and noise removal, sharpening, color, upscaling and extra frames. Face restoration is not included yet — none of the models tested were good enough.

Privacy

Yes! When using local on-device models or Ollama on your network, no data leaves your device or local network. Private mode with biometric lock adds extra security for sensitive conversations.

Ari Helper is built with multiple layers of privacy and security protections to give you full control over your data:

  • On-device models — Run AI models entirely on your device using Apple's MLX framework. Your prompts and responses never leave your device, giving you complete privacy with zero external dependencies.
  • Fortress Mode — Lock down the app to only allow connections to secure, vetted model platforms. When enabled, Ari Helper restricts which providers can be used, ensuring your sensitive prompts are only sent to trusted endpoints. IT administrators can also enforce Fortress Mode across an organization via Apple MDM profiles.
  • Confirmation dialogs — Before sending prompts to external providers, Ari Helper can present confirmation dialogs so you always have a chance to review what's being sent and to which provider before any data leaves your device.
  • Encrypted Private Conversations — Sensitive chats can be encrypted and locked behind biometric authentication (Face ID or Touch ID). Private conversations are encrypted on device, so even if someone has access to your device they cannot read them without your biometric approval.
  • Encrypted local sync — When a conversation moves directly between your own devices over Wi-Fi, a private conversation crosses the link as exactly the encrypted bytes it is on disk; nothing is decrypted in transit, and the key that opens it never leaves your iCloud keychain. The link itself is TLS, and only devices signed in to your own iCloud account can complete the handshake.

Whether you're running models entirely on device, connecting to a self-hosted Ollama instance on your network, or using a cloud provider — Ari Helper gives you the tools to choose the privacy level that's right for each conversation.
