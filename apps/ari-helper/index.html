The idea

Your AI should work for you, not the other way round.

Most AI apps choose the model, keep your conversations and charge by the month. Ari does the opposite. It runs the model you pick — one on your iPhone, one on the Mac under your desk, or one from any provider you have a key for — and keeps what you say on your devices.

Ari 7 applies the same idea to pictures and film. Take a stranger out of a holiday photograph, repair a scan of your grandparents' wedding, give an old broadcast its detail back. The models that do it download once, then run on your device.

02Photo Studio

Brush over it. Watch it go.

Photo Studio is a canvas editor with on-device models behind it. Paint over what's wrong and pick a tool, or select nothing and treat the whole photograph. Every step is kept, so going back three edits takes one tap.

Everything below was produced by Ari on public-domain pictures that ship with the app. Where a picture had nothing to fix, the flaw was added first, so you can compare the result with the real original. Open "See what each tool does" in Ari to run them yourself.

03Film Studio

Write a film one shot at a time.

Film Studio is laid out like an editing suite. Write a film as a list of shots, render each with the video model you choose, keep the takes that work, and watch the cut come together above the timeline. Films sync through iCloud, so a scene started on the Mac is there on the iPhone.

Write

Describe each shot and how long it runs. Narration and a still to start from are optional.

Render

Each shot is rendered on its own, with the settings its model needs, and shows a live preview while it works.

Cut

Keep the good takes, drop the rest, and play the film from the first shot to the last.

Apollo 11 television — NASA, 1969, shown at twice its size. Grain was removed across frames, then noise: the fine speckle drops by about a third, and the picture keeps its tone.

Clean up any video, not just your own.

The Clean up menu runs a whole restoration pass on one shot, on the finished cut, or on any video file you open. Ari runs each step in the order that works best.

Ari Helper is built with multiple layers of privacy and security protections to give you full control over your data:

  • On-device models — Run AI models entirely on your device using Apple's MLX framework. Your prompts and responses never leave your device, giving you complete privacy with zero external dependencies.
  • Fortress Mode — Lock down the app to only allow connections to secure, vetted model platforms. When enabled, Ari Helper restricts which providers can be used, ensuring your sensitive prompts are only sent to trusted endpoints. IT administrators can also enforce Fortress Mode across an organization via Apple MDM profiles.
  • Confirmation dialogs — Before sending prompts to external providers, Ari Helper can present confirmation dialogs so you always have a chance to review what's being sent and to which provider before any data leaves your device.
  • Encrypted Private Conversations — Sensitive chats can be encrypted and locked behind biometric authentication (Face ID or Touch ID). Private conversations are encrypted on device, so even if someone has access to your device they cannot read them without your biometric approval.
  • Encrypted local sync — When a conversation moves directly between your own devices over Wi-Fi, a private conversation crosses the link as exactly the encrypted bytes it is on disk; nothing is decrypted in transit, and the key that opens it never leaves your iCloud keychain. The link itself is TLS, and only devices signed in to your own iCloud account can complete the handshake.

Whether you're running models entirely on device, connecting to a self-hosted Ollama instance on your network, or using a cloud provider — Ari Helper gives you the tools to choose the privacy level that's right for each conversation.
