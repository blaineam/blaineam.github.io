Your AI should work for you, not the other way round.

01Chat

Any model. One conversation.

Start a question with Apple Intelligence, send the follow-up to Claude, and ask a model on your own Mac for a second opinion — in the same thread, with the same attachments.

02Look

Point the camera. Ask out loud.

Look at Something turns the camera into a conversation. Ari sends what you see to the model you've chosen, captions the answer under the picture, and reads it to you.

03Photo Studio

Brush over it. Watch it go.

Paint over what's wrong and pick a tool: remove, repair, denoise, colorize or enlarge. The restoration models run on your device.

04Film Studio

Write a film one shot at a time.

Write a film as a list of shots, render each with the video model you choose, and keep the takes that work. Clean up restores any video you open.

05Everywhere

Start on your wrist. Finish at your desk.

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, and conversations sync through iCloud. Dictate on your wrist; let the Mac take the heavy work.
