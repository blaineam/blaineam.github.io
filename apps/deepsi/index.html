DeepSi

See depth, not light.

DeepSi turns the depth sensors in your iPhone or iPad into a camera that captures shape instead of light.

Download on the App Store
Download on App Store

Ever wondered what the world would look like if you saw depth instead of light?

01Night vision

It sees in the dark

The depth sensors measure distance with their own infrared light, so DeepSi keeps working in a pitch-black room. Use it as a night-vision viewfinder without lighting anything up.

02Color

Swipe to recolor

Swipe left or right to move through twelve color themes. They follow your Light or Dark Mode setting, and DeepSi remembers the one you picked.

03Capture

Tap for a photo. Hold for a video.

Depth photos and depth videos with sound, saved to a DeepSi album in your Photos library.
