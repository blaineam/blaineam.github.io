What's New in Embr

Widgets, an Apple Watch app, Siri & Shortcuts, and Spotlight search

🧩

Home & Lock Screen Widgets

Keep your payoff progress and next payment due in sight with home screen and lock screen widgets. They surface in the Smart Stack around payment due dates and tap straight into the app.

⌚

Embr on Apple Watch

Payoff progress rings for your total and each debt, a Smart Stack complication, and on-wrist payment logging — pick a debt, dial in the amount with the Digital Crown, confirm. Syncs through the same encrypted iCloud pipeline as the iPhone app, no phone required nearby.

🗣️

Siri & Shortcuts

Log a payment, check a debt's balance or payoff date, get your total debt, and read or update your extra-payment settings — all from Shortcuts and Siri, ready for your own automations.

🔍

Debts in Spotlight

Your debts are indexed with Spotlight on-device, so searching "car loan" finds the actual debt and jumps you straight to it.
