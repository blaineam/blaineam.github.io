Frequently Asked Questions

Every question about Embr, answered.

Embr is a debt payoff estimation tool that helps you visualize when you'll be debt-free. Enter your debts and try various payment schedules to see how different scenarios affect your timeline. The burn visualization makes tracking progress satisfying and motivating.

No! Embr collects zero data. Everything runs on your device with complete privacy. No tracking, no servers, no sharing your financial information with anyone. Your debt details stay completely private.

Embr supports various payment schedules to help you estimate different debt payoff scenarios. Adjust payment amounts and frequencies to find the plan that works best for your budget and timeline.

Embr's payoff math was checked by an accountant, and it factors in each debt's interest rate and any extra payments. Treat the dates as a plan to aim for, since real balances change as you pay.

No! Embr is a one-time purchase that works across all your Apple devices—iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Buy once and use forever with no recurring subscription fees.

Your debt plans and progress sync seamlessly across all your Apple devices via iCloud. Monitor your debt payoff journey from your iPhone, check progress on your iPad, or plan at your Mac—always in sync.

Embr's unique burn visualization shows your debts literally burning away as you make payments. The visual feedback is oddly satisfying and keeps you motivated throughout your debt-free journey. Simple and clean UI makes the whole experience enjoyable.

Yes. Embr is on the App Store everywhere in the European Union, France included. It's available in every App Store territory Apple offers.
