Free is a real app, not a demo

There is no length limit on the free tier. Paste a whole article, import a whole book, and listen to all of it with any voice on your device — no trial countdown, no truncation, no paywall in the middle of a chapter. Free keeps three saved speeches, each with its own place marked.

Pricing questions

Monthly and Yearly both start with a 7-day free trial. You aren't charged until it ends, and cancelling before then costs nothing. Apple allows one trial per Apple Account.

In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Mi Speaks. Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for, and everything free keeps working after that.

Yes. Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime all work with Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group gets Premium.

Open Premium in Mi Speaks and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
