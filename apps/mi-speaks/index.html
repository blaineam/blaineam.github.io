What's New in Mi Speaks

Spotlight search, Shortcuts audio rendering, a Resume Reading widget, and an Apple Watch reader

🔍

Find Speeches in Spotlight

Saved speeches are indexed by their content, so a phrase you remember finds the speech. Tap a result and you land right back in it, ready to play.

🗣️

Shortcuts & Siri

Import text, PDFs, or photos (with text recognition) into saved speeches; play, pause, and resume from automations; switch voices and playback speed; render any text to an audio file fully offline; and export a sentence range as audio — every action chainable in Shortcuts.

🧩

Resume Reading Widget

Home screen and lock screen widgets show your last speech and how far you got, and tap straight back into playback. Surfaces in the Smart Stack while a speech is partially read.

⌚

Mi Speaks on Apple Watch

A standalone watch reader with background audio — through the speaker or your Bluetooth headphones — that highlights each word as it's spoken. Progress syncs both ways, so your iPhone and watch resume each other seamlessly.
