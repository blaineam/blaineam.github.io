Read along

Transform any written content into natural-sounding speech. Import text from images, PDFs, web pages, or type directly into the app. Mi Speaks makes all your content accessible through audio.

  • 📖

    Your book, on its own page

    Import a PDF and read it where it actually lives: the real page, the real layout, with the spoken word lit up exactly where it sits. Turn it off and you are back to plain sentences — it is a mode, not a takeover.

  • 📍

    Highlight & Follow

    Follow along with highlighted text as Mi Speaks reads, making it easier to read and comprehend content.

  • 🔖

    Jump to any chapter

    Mi Speaks reads a PDF's own table of contents, and when a book has none it finds the headings from the page layout. A four-hundred-page import becomes a list you can move around in — and the skip buttons on your lock screen move chapter by chapter.

  • 📄

    Import from Anywhere

    Share a PDF from Files, Safari or Mail and it opens as the real document, with its pages, highlighting and chapters. Paste text from any app, or photograph a page and Mi Speaks reads the words off it.

  • 💡

    Helpful Tips

    Built-in helpful tips guide you through features and help you get the most out of Mi Speaks.

Listen anywhere

Perfect for reading articles while commuting, listening to documents while working out, or helping with accessibility needs.

  • ⏯️

    Background Playback

    Keep listening in another app or with the screen locked, with play, pause and skip on the lock screen. If a Studio Voice passage isn't prepared yet, it carries on in your own system voice — the reading never just stops.

  • ✈️

    Ready before you are

    Prepare a whole document once and every sentence is already rendered to audio. It keeps playing with the screen locked, in a tunnel, on a plane. Your place follows you to your iPad and Apple Watch, and prepared documents are sent to the watch on their own — so it reads them in the same Studio Voice.

    In-app purchase
  • 📚

    Your library, everywhere

    Keep as many documents as you like, each with its place marked, in sync across iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch through your iCloud Drive.

    In-app purchase
  • 🎧

    Export to an audio file

Nothing leaves your device

Every voice runs locally. Your text is never uploaded, never sent to a server to be spoken, and never needs an account — which also means Mi Speaks works the same in airplane mode as it does on Wi-Fi.

  • 🔒

    Zero Data Collection

    Complete privacy—all processing happens on your device. No data is collected, tracked, or sent anywhere. Affordable and privacy-focused.
