Frequently Asked Questions

Every well-supported rclone backend — Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, pCloud, Mega, Backblaze B2, Azure, Google Cloud Storage and S3-compatible storage among them — plus FTP, SFTP, WebDAV and SMB. You can also drop in an rclone.conf you already have.

Enter Space needs an in-app purchase to use: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Every feature is included in each, new subscribers start with a 7-day free trial, and Family Sharing is supported.

Enter Space uses Apple's File Provider framework to integrate directly with the Files app on iOS and Finder on macOS. Your cloud storage appears as a location in Files/Finder, allowing you to access files with any app that supports file browsing.

While Enter Space can connect to over 100 cloud storage providers, the app runs entirely on your device. It connects directly from the app to your configured providers — Enter Space does not monitor, track, or even route your data through any intermediary servers. This means Enter Space has zero insight into how you use the app or what data you store, ensuring the utmost privacy.

The Serve feature lets you spin up a local server that exposes any of your configured remotes using a variety of Rclone serve types — including WebDAV, NFS, FTP, SFTP, HTTP, S3, DLNA, and Restic. This is useful for accessing your cloud files through other apps or devices on your network.

On a Mac, the Mount Helper can mount WebDAV, FTP and NFS serves in Finder.
