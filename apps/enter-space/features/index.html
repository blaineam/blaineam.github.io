Connect & mount

  • 🗂️

    Mount every cloud like a local drive

    Enter Space integrates directly with macOS Finder and iOS Files app through File Provider technology. Access all 100 cloud storage providers as if they were native drives on your device.

    Drag and drop files, use Spotlight search, and work with your cloud files using any app—just like they're stored locally. Full read and write support for the majority of supported clouds.

  • ☁️

    100 Cloud Providers

    Connect to virtually any cloud storage service including Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud, S3, and 95 more providers all in one place.

  • 🗄️

    Unified Storage Hub

    Cloud remotes and local folders now live together in one tidy Storage list — combined actions, per-item encryption toggles, and a one-tap sort that flips between alphabetical and most-recently-used.

  • 🧭

    Guided Remote Setup

    Add and edit remote configurations through a simple, guided visual interface. No command line required—get connected in just a few taps.

  • 🌍

    Serve Configuration

    Host your remote content locally with the Serve feature supporting WebDAV, NFS, FTP, S3, SFTP, HTTP, DLNA, and Restic. NFS is significantly faster than WebDAV for media playback over encrypted cloud remotes because macOS's NFS client skips the per-file metadata probes Finder's WebDAV client does.

  • 💻

    Mount Helper App

    The optional Enter Space Mount Helper App auto-mounts a WebDAV, FTP, or NFS Serve as a local folder with zero password prompts. NFS mounts land under ~/Library/Application Support/Enter Space/Mounts/ instead of /Volumes/ so Spotlight doesn't try to index your entire cloud tree.

    ⬇ Download Helper
  • 🔒

    File Locking Support

    Lock files on supported cloud providers to prevent accidental edits or deletions. Keep critical files safe with collaborative locking support.

  • ⭐

    Favorited Folders

    Pin your most-used folders for instant access. Favorite folders stay front and center across all your devices for quick navigation.

  • 📊

    Storage Space at a Glance

    Every storage location now carries a thin used/free capacity bar — cloud remotes and linked local folders alike — color-coded as space runs low, with the full readout a long-press away. Make it yours in Settings: hide the bars or switch them to a calm monochrome.

  • ✨

    Streamlined Menu Bar & Home

    A refreshed macOS menu-bar experience: a quick-add button for new storage, Settings and Help in their own windows that stay out of your way, and a Home tab that puts favorites, recently played media, and scheduled syncs front and center on every device.

  • 💽

    Mount a remote as a real volume

    On a Mac, a file-system extension mounts a remote as a real volume that Finder and Terminal both see. Switch a remote on or off and the volume updates in place.

    Mac
  • ✍️

    Rename many files at once

    Select two or more files and build the new names from a pattern — the name, a counter, the extension — or find and replace, with a live preview that flags collisions before anything changes.

Photos & media

  • 🖼️

    One photo library, laid over every cloud

    An Apple-Photos-style library over any of your remotes: year/month/day timeline, Places map, Albums, Favorites, and full-text search. Live Photos play on hold, RAW files open as true RAW develops, and 360° shots get an interactive look-around viewer. Everything indexes into a private, encrypted on-device library that syncs to your other devices serverlessly — and any photo, video, or album can be downloaded for fully offline use, encrypted at rest.

  • 🍿

    A private Theater built from files you already own

    Point Theater at your movie and TV folders and get Netflix-style shelves with artwork and details from TMDB — or no key at all, and it builds your library from your file names. Continue Watching follows you across iPhone, Mac, and Apple TV; episodes auto-advance; resume never spoils. A custom player built for real-world files means audio and subtitle track choices actually switch and are remembered — across resume, episodes, launches, and devices. Download whole seasons for offline viewing, encrypted on-device.

  • 🎬

    Built-in Media Player

    View photos, watch videos, and listen to audio directly from your cloud storage with background playback support. Video plays in Enter Space's own player — Liquid Glass controls, AirPlay, playback speed, and audio/subtitle menus that work reliably with multi-track files. Your media, always accessible.

  • 🌐

    360° & 180° Media Playback

    Immerse yourself in 360° and 180° photos and videos streamed directly from your cloud storage. Explore panoramic and spatial media with intuitive controls.

  • 📺

    Apple TV Playback

    Stream your cloud-stored media directly to Apple TV. Enjoy your videos, photos, and audio on the big screen with seamless AirPlay integration.

  • 🔓

    Approve Apple TV Unlocks from Your Phone

    Hidden remotes stay hidden on the Apple TV by default. When someone taps "Show Hidden Remotes" on the TV, your iPhone or Mac gets a push notification with Approve / Deny buttons — even if the app's force-quit. Tap Approve, Face ID, done. Each TV is paired by name so an imposter device can't trick you into approving the wrong one. Tap Lock from the phone and the TV snaps back to the locked home grid instantly, even mid-playback.

  • 🔃

    Recently Played Syncing

    Pick up where you left off on any device. Your recently played media syncs across iPhone, iPad, and Mac so you never lose your place.

  • 🎞️

    SWF Media Playback

    Play legacy Flash SWF files directly within the app. Revisit classic Flash content and animations without needing a separate player or browser plugin.

Sync, backup & recovery

  • ⏱️

    Set the schedule once, then stop thinking about backups

    Schedule automated sync operations with advanced filtering and incremental backups — a whole folder, or a single file. Supports near realtime file change monitoring so your data syncs moments after it changes. Keep your data safe across all devices with smart, efficient transfers.

  • 🕰️

    Rewind any folder to any moment

    Browse through your version history from any snapshot's point of view. Restore files and folders as needed with intuitive time-travel navigation.

  • 📸

    Photos Library Backup

    Back up your entire Photos library to any cloud provider. Protect your precious memories with automated, secure photo backups.

  • 🔋

    Smart Sync Scheduling

    Automatically skip scheduled syncs when on battery power or connected to low-data or costly networks. Save battery life and avoid unexpected data charges.

  • 📌

    Keep Offline

    Pin any folder of any remote and Enter Space keeps a complete local copy — encrypted at rest — that refreshes automatically in the background. When the remote can't be reached, you can still browse and preview the downloaded copy, with a per-pin Wi-Fi Only toggle to protect your data plan.

  • 🧹

    Storage Cleanup & Space Inspector

    Drill into any cloud or local storage location and see exactly which folders are eating your space. Sorted biggest first, recursive multi-select to clear it all out in one pass.

  • 🪞

    Duplicate Photo, Video & Audio Finder

    Spot near-identical photos, videos, and audio across every connected cloud. Sees through resizes, format swaps, and re-edits — and warns you when one looks watermarked so you keep the clean original.

Security & privacy

  • 🔐

    Encrypted before it ever leaves your device

    Your files are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded to the cloud. Enter Space uses end-to-end encryption to ensure only you can access your data, even if the cloud provider is compromised.

    Built on Rclone's proven open-source technology with years of community testing and security audits. Fast, reliable, and secure cloud access you can trust.

  • 🔐

    Secure Drop

    Share files and folders with end-to-end encryption built on Rclone's Crypt. Data is encrypted before it leaves your device, and the recipient decrypts it using a web-based decryption tool with a password you share outside of the storage provider — adding an extra layer of privacy even if the shared link is compromised.

  • ☁️

    iCloud-Optional Privacy

    Default-on iCloud sync replicates your rclone configs, SSH keys, and Personal Access Tokens across your Apple devices. Prefer device-only? One toggle keeps every new credential local.

  • 🔗

    Public Link Creation

    Generate shareable public links for files and folders on supported cloud providers. Share your content instantly without leaving the app.

  • ⚡

    Powered by Rclone

    Built on the proven open-source Rclone CLI with a track record of reliability. Fast, efficient, and communicates directly with cloud providers.

Developer tools

  • ✏️

    Full-Featured Code Editor

    Edit code with syntax highlighting, code folding, comprehensive find and replace, draft versioning, and follow mode for watching logs in real-time.

  • 🌿

    Git in the Code Editor

    Stage, commit, push, pull, branch — full Git workflow built into the editor. SSH keys on Mac and HTTPS auto-token on iPhone, so the same SSH-form remote URL just works on every device.

  • 🪄

    Git Mirror

    Pull a remote folder down as a local Git working copy, edit with your normal toolchain, push the changes back to the cloud. No mount required, no manual sync.

  • 👁️

    Live Web Preview

    Edit a webpage in the editor and watch it render live alongside — every script, image, and stylesheet loading like the real thing. Auto-refreshes the moment you save.

  • 💾

    Auto-Saving Editor Drafts

    Switch tabs, close the editor, force-quit the app — your unsaved work is captured automatically and waiting for you next time. No "discard changes?" surprises.

  • 🔧

    Custom Environment Variables

    Set custom environment variables for fine-grained control over Rclone behavior and cloud provider configurations. Tailor your setup to match advanced workflows.

  • ⚙️

    Rclone CLI Integration

    Optionally share your remote configurations with the rclone command line through a shared rclone.conf file. Power users can seamlessly switch between the visual interface and terminal workflows.

  • 🛠️

    In-App Browser Debug Console

    Inspect and debug web content with a built-in developer console for the in-app browser. View logs, errors, and network activity without leaving the app.

Across your devices

  • ⌚️

    Enter Space on Apple Watch

    Sync your cloud media to your wrist. A media-first Apple Watch companion browses your synced library and plays it back right from the watch — with background audio so your music keeps going. A watch-face photo complication flips through a folder you choose (tap it to open that photo, turn the Digital Crown to zoom), and it adapts to tinted faces so your photos stay legible. Everything's stored encrypted at rest, with the media key delivered securely from your paired iPhone.

  • ⌚️

    Smarter Apple Watch Sync

    Slideshows advance on their own when a track ends, look-alike photos are de-duplicated before they reach your wrist, VR/360 media is skipped (the watch can't project it), and watch copies are never larger than the original. Background transfers pause while you're watching or listening, so playback stays smooth.

  • 🧩

    Home & Lock Screen Widgets

    Four starfield-styled widgets for iPhone, iPad, and Mac: a Photo Slideshow of your favorites, a Storage Dashboard with per-location used/free bars and provider icons, a Bandwidth monitor, and a Sync Status board for your scheduled syncs. Tap any one to jump straight into the app — a widget photo opens that exact frame in the gallery slideshow, a sync opens its run-results detail.

  • 🗣️

    24 Shortcuts & Siri Actions

    Enter Space's whole feature surface, automatable: copy, move, rename, upload, download, and share files; recover versions through Time Portal; run Doppel duplicate scans and Space Inspector reports; commit and push Git changes; create and run scheduled syncs — all chainable, with time, location, and Focus triggers via Shortcuts automations. Secrets can never be read or written.

  • ☁️

    Cross-Device iCloud Sync

    Your Apple Watch favorites and hidden items, your recently-played list, and your exact playback position now follow you from device to device through your private iCloud — pick a track or photo back up right where you left off.

  • 🔎

    Spotlight Full-Content File Search

    Find files by what's INSIDE them, straight from system Spotlight — text, Markdown, source code, and PDFs in local sources are content-indexed, and cloud sources are searchable by name. Privacy is built in: hidden content is never indexed and encrypted remotes stay out of Spotlight by default, with per-source toggles in Settings.

  • 📤

    Open Anywhere

    Right-click a file in Finder or share from any app — Enter Space opens it in the code editor, gallery, or sends it via Secure Drop. Native share extensions on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
