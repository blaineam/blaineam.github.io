Fastest Right Answer Wins

Both players get the same question on the same clock. Answer first and answer right, and the draw is yours.

Flip, Bank, or Bust

Flip cards from a shared 3×3 grid to bank 1, 2 or 3 points, then lock your pot or push on. Two Zaps hide in the grid, and one wipes the round.
