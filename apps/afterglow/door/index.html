    Designed for the bigger shell

    Made for the Afterglow shell

    These doors fit the Afterglow remix, 7 mm wider and 12 mm longer than the original viewer. They use its hinge and latch, and they won't fit the original shell.

    No TPU, one print

    Rigid anchors printed into the door locate the phone at each corner. There are no soft bumpers to print, change filament for or lose.

    A front stop for every size

    Pro Max phones seat on the shell's screen shelves. Smaller Pro phones pass inside those shelves, so their doors add four rigid clips that hold the edge of the glass.

    MagSafe where the magnets are

    Every position comes from Apple's drawing for that phone. It isn't always the middle: on the iPhone 17 Pro and 18 Pro the magnets sit 2.7 mm toward the USB end.

    Printing it

    • Print in PETG, outer face down, exactly as the file is oriented. No supports.
    • Use a 0.2 mm layer or finer so the MagSafe groove comes out clean.
    • Hang the door on the shell with its hinge pin and close it with a hair tie, as usual.
    • On a clipped door, press the phone in screen up until all four clips catch. To take it out, push through the dots from the back.

    Every door here is checked against the shell's CAD for overlaps. The iPhone 18 Pro Max door shares the design that has been printed. The clips on the smaller phones haven't been printed yet, so try the fit gently on your first one. Print the shell from MakerWorld.
