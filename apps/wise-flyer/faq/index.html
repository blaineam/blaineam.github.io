Wise Flyer

Frequently Asked Questions

Every question about Wise Flyer, answered.

Simply tap the screen to make the owl flap and fly upward. Release to let gravity bring you down. Navigate through obstacles by timing your taps carefully. It's easy to learn but challenging to master!

The global leaderboard tracks high scores from players around the world. Your best score is automatically submitted, and you can see where you rank against other players. Climb the ranks by improving your skills!

Wise Flyer is inspired by the classic bird flying game genre but features unique graphics with a charming owl character, distinctive visual style, and its own personality. If you loved those games, you'll love this!

Yes! Wise Flyer can be played completely offline. Leaderboard features require an internet connection, but the core gameplay works anywhere, anytime.

Yes! The scrolling is infinite and obstacles are continuously generated. The game only ends when you hit an obstacle. How far can you fly? The only limit is your skill!

Beautiful unique graphics featuring an adorable owl, smooth gameplay, an amazing soundtrack, competitive leaderboard, and that perfect "just one more try" addictiveness. It's simple, fun, and incredibly hard to put down!

Yes. Wise Flyer runs on iPhone and iPad, and the game scales to fit any window, including Split View and Stage Manager.
