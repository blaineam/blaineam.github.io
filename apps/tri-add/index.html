Frequently Asked Questions

Light (additive) mixing starts from black and adds toward white — red light + green light makes yellow. Paint (subtractive) mixing starts from white and multiplies toward black — cyan + yellow makes green. Tri-Add teaches both, then flips between them mid-game.

There's no timer — the colors get subtler as your level climbs and the preview "help" fades — two peeks early, then one, then none. A built-in perceptual floor keeps every color visibly distinct, even at the top.
