A night sky you can walk around in

Tonight's real sky, with one thing in front of you at a time: a gallery of photographs with a slideshow, panoramas you can stand inside, the apps I build, and my experience as a timeline. Or choose Sky only and use it as a planetarium.

  • Look drag with a mouse or finger, or use the arrow keys
  • Select click, tap, or press Enter; Tab moves through the items
  • Go back Escape. Open an area 1, 2, 3, 4. Page comma and full stop. Slideshow Space
  • In a headset, hands only point and pinch to select, or push a button with a fingertip. Everything stays put unless you drag the Move handle on the control bar, which swings it wherever you like, overhead included
  • Controllers or a simple viewer point and pull the trigger; or just look at something until the ring fills
  • The sky is tonight's, for roughly where you are. Point at a planet, the moon or the sun to see what it is
  • Passthrough where the headset has it, a button inside VR swaps the painted sky for your own room
