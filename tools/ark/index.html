ARK

From green tests to every store

🦉Soren

Nothing ships red

Soren runs every test suite on every platform, from Xcode to Rust, Gradle and Node. A release waits until the whole board is green.

🦊Knox

A security review on every release

Knox reads the real source, domain by domain, from the threat model to the supply chain, and ranks what it finds. A new high-severity finding stops the line.

🐵🐆Monkr & Levi

Framed, and fluent in eight languages

Monkr turns fresh captures into framed store screenshots. Levi fills every gap in the Big 8 languages, then proves nothing is missing.

🦝Rocket

One command, three stores

Rocket drafts the release, pushes the listing, uploads the build and submits it to the App Store, Google Play and the Microsoft Store.

🐰Hopps · New

Then it watches, and loops back

Hopps gathers what the stores, the website and social channels report into one private dashboard. Every ticket and suggestion has a Fix with Claude button.
