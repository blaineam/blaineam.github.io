Tom · Music machine

Music in seconds. Yours to keep.

Dial in a melody, snap a song together like Lego, or tune in to endless radio. Royalty-free, and it all runs in your browser.

Open Tom

Free and open source (MIT)·Source on GitHub

Every note comes from a seed, so the same seed always makes the same music.

01Melody Machine

A melody, dialed in

Pick a style, key, tempo, swing and shape, and Tom writes the melody. Turning a dial keeps the seed, so you shape it instead of starting over.

02Composer

Songs that snap together

Drop intro, verse, chorus and ending blocks on a timeline, or let Auto build, finish or re-roll whatever you haven't locked.

03Radio

Radio that never repeats

Pick a station and Tom writes an endless run of new songs. On iPhone it keeps playing with the screen locked.

04Royalty-free

No samples. No claims.

Tom writes every note itself, so there's nothing to license and nothing to get flagged. A share link's hashtag is the whole song.

05Command line

On the command line, too

The same engine runs in Node: render WAV and MIDI from a seed, or score a Monkr video with a jingle that lands on the beat.
